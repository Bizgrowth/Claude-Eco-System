Dental reminder tools: competitor notes

RemindRight (fictional)

Sends SMS reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before an appointment. Claims to cut no-shows by 25%. Pricing is per message. Staff dashboard shows "confirmed / unconfirmed / no reply".

ChairFull (fictional)

Email-first reminders with one-click confirm. Waitlist feature fills cancelled slots automatically. Weak mobile experience according to reviews.

Common patterns

  • Reminder timing is configurable, usually 24-72 hours ahead.
  • Staff want a single "needs follow-up" list rather than raw logs.
  • Patients dislike receiving more than two reminders.
